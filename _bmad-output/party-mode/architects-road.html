The Architect's Road

You start as an apprentice with an empty workshop. Each week you add one real piece to your own small city of servers (the Citadel), watch it work, break it on purpose, and repair it. By the end you can design systems from scratch and you have the skills to build your own simulator.

The four oaths

  1. Predict before you press. Write what you expect, then run it.
  2. First 30 minutes alone. No AI and no search on a new problem.
  3. AI is your opponent, not your author. Ask it to attack your design, never to write it.
  4. Design first, read the answer after. The gap between them is the lesson.

Your Guide

An old architect who tells each lesson as a chapter of your story. Each week a named Foe attacks your Citadel. The Guide sets the scene, asks for your prediction, sends you on missions with real tools, then fights your design in the Boss battle. You'll get questions and hints, never finished answers.
