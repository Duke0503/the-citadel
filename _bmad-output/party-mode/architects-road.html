The Architect's Road

You start as an apprentice with an empty workshop. Each week you add one real piece to your own small city of servers (the Citadel), watch it work, break it on purpose, and repair it. By the end you can design systems from scratch and build your own simulator.

A week, in seven beats

  • Mon – TueScene, Prediction, Codex. Commit to a guess, then learn the theory and read.
  • WedWatch it run in a visualizer or step-through lesson.
  • Thu – FriBuild it in the Citadel, then break it and measure.
  • WeekendFace the Boss, then the Debrief and field log.

The four oaths

  1. Predict before you press. Write what you expect, then run it.
  2. First 30 minutes alone. No AI and no search on a new problem.
  3. AI is your opponent, not your author. Ask it to attack your design, never to write it.
  4. Design first, read the answer after. The gap between them is the lesson.
